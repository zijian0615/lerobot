# Parts sheet (from the parts supplier -- reference only)

The parts this cell sorts, as delivered. Dimensions and the robot interface
are as given in TASK.md.

## AA alkaline cell (battery)

- 14.5 mm across, 50.5 mm long with its terminal button.
- Plastic film wrap, in any of the supplier's colours, printed
  `AA 1.5V ALKALINE` along the axis, with a printed band round the wrap at the
  + end.
- The print reads along the axis. Every cell carries the printed band at its + end, so the band is how to tell a cell from a pin: a 50 mm cylinder with no band is a steel dowel pin.
- + end: a small raised steel button inside a black insulating ring.
  - end: a flat steel disc.

## Steel dowel pin

- 14.5 mm across, 50.5 mm long: the same size as a cell.
- Bare, plated or painted in any of the same colours; no print.
- Both end faces flat, with a small chamfer.

## Plastic sleeve

- 15 mm across, 45 mm long, a 9 mm bore through it.
- Glossy or matte, in any of the same colours.

## Notes

- Colour is not a clue: every part type comes in every colour.
