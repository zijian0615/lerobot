# Task: mount the pressure transmitter (assembly.instrument)

Write `program.py` defining `solve(env)`.

The PT-200 pressure transmitter stands on the aluminium mounting plate. Fix it
to the plate with the four M3 screws from the feeder: one screw in each of the
four holes in its mounting flange, driven with the arm's electric
screwdriver. Tighten each screw to the maximum torque given on the
instrument's nameplate (on the top of its housing, next to the display). Do
not damage the instrument: keep the driver and the screws off its housing and
display window, and do not press on its signal cable. If a step cannot be
done safely, do not force it.
